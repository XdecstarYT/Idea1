Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Marshall Web Co. (ABN 00 000 000 000) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we look after it, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, business name, phone number, email address, website address and anything you write in a message.
  • Business contact details that are publicly listed (for example on Google Maps or your website), which we may use to contact you about our services.
  • Basic, anonymous website statistics (such as pages visited), if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare mockups, quotes and proposals.
  • To deliver and support the services you buy from us.
  • To send invoices and service updates.
  • To send occasional marketing messages, only where the law allows it. Every marketing email or text includes a way to unsubscribe, and we act on unsubscribe requests within 5 business days.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We only share it with service providers we use to run the business (such as our form, email, hosting and invoicing providers), and only as needed to provide our services. Some of these providers may store data outside Australia.

Do Not Call

If you'd like us to stop calling you, just tell us and we'll add your number to our do-not-contact list. We check numbers against the Australian Do Not Call Register before making marketing calls.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at hello@marshallweb.com.au.

Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
