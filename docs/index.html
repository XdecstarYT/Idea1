Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Something else on your mind? Email me and I'll get back to you within a day.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I design your homepage before you commit to anything. If it's not right for you, you don't pay and you don't owe me anything.

How does the $0 upfront option work?

Instead of paying for the website in one go, you pay a monthly fee that also covers hosting, security and small changes. There's a 12-month minimum, then it's month to month.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Absolutely. Run the free website check above, or I'll review it for you and tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild, a tidy-up, or is fine as it is.

Do I own my website?

If you pay upfront, yes, it's yours. On monthly plans you own your domain and content, and you can buy out the design at any time.

I'm not good with tech. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what I'm for. You answer a short questionnaire and send some photos, and I handle the rest. Changes are as easy as sending a message.

How long does it take?

Your free mockup takes about 3 days. Once you go ahead, Starter sites are live in about 7 days and Growth in about 14, depending on how quickly we get your photos and details.

Do you only work with Warrnambool businesses?

I'm based in Warrnambool and focus on South West Victoria, from Port Fairy to Camperdown and beyond, but I'm happy to help businesses anywhere in Australia.
