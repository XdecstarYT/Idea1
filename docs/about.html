Why I started.

Drive down any street in Warrnambool and you'll find great businesses: plumbers who turn up on time, cafés with the best coffee on the coast, salons booked out by word of mouth. Then search for them on Google, and half of them barely exist online.

Big agencies charge thousands and talk in jargon. DIY website builders eat up your evenings. I sit in the middle: a proper, professional website at a price that makes sense for a small business, built by someone local who actually gets back to you.

Hi, I'm Declan Marshall. Yes, the Marshall in Marshall Web Co. is me. I'll be the one you talk to, the one who builds your site, and the one you message when you need a change.
