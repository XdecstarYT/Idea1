The story behind SKOCKZ

Skateboarding has always been about bringing people together. But when injury strikes, isolation can hit hard: you're stuck on the sidelines, watching your crew progress while you heal.

That feeling inspired SKOCKZ. Our Youth Isolation Focus is about keeping you connected to skate culture, even when you can't ride. Bold designs, hot pink accents and street-inspired graphics that remind you you're still part of the crew.

Every SKOCKZ order comes with a free sticker, and extra stickers are just $1 each.
