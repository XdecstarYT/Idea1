The story behind SKOCKZ

Made by a skater,
for the skate community.

SKOCKZ started in a garage in Warrnambool with a simple idea: the gear you rep should feel just as personal as the culture behind it.

Why I started SKOCKZ

I'm a Year 12 student and a skater. I started SKOCKZ because the skatepark is where mates are made, tricks are learned, and everything else disappears for a while.

When an injury put me on the sidelines, I felt how isolating it is to be cut off from that. Filming my mates and slowly building back got me through it, and that's the spirit this brand is built on.

Ordering & how it's made

Is it actually handmade?

Actually handmade. Every clay SKOCKSTER, keychain and logo piece is rolled, shaped and assembled by hand in Warrnambool. No moulds, no casts. Yours will vary a little from the photos, and that's what makes it one of one.

Can I choose the colour?

Yep. Pick your options on the product page. Most clay pieces come in 8 colours, and several have a resin glaze upgrade for a glossy, harder-wearing finish.

Is it made before or after I order?

After. Clay pieces are made once your order comes through, so they take a few days. Stickers and anything in stock go out straight away.

Do I get free stickers?

A free sticker sheet goes in every single order.

What's the FREE BONUS KEYCHAIN banner?

If you see the gold banner on a product page, a second handmade keychain goes in your parcel at no cost. It's a surprise, and it's a real one.

Returns, sizing & payment

It arrived faulty or damaged

Email sskockzz@gmail.com within 30 days with your order number and a photo. We'll sort a replacement, repair or refund, and cover return postage on faulty items. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law always apply.

I changed my mind. Can I return it?

Yes, on most things: send back an unused item in its original condition within 30 days for a refund. Pieces made specifically for you (clay SKOCKSTERS, keychains, custom colours, made-to-order tees) can't be returned for change of mind.

What size should I get?

Socks are one size, AU 8–12. For everything else, measure a shirt you already love and compare it with the size guide, or email us and we'll tell you straight.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and the express wallets at checkout. Checkout runs on Shopify's secure checkout, so SKOCKZ never sees your card details.
