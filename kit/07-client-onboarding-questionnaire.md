# 07 · Client onboarding questionnaire

Send this as a **Google Form** (free). Copy the questions in, then email the link the day they sign. It takes them about 10 minutes and gives you everything you need to build.

---

## About your business
1. Business name (exactly as it should appear on the site)
2. Your name and role
3. Best phone number for customers to call
4. Best email for enquiries to go to
5. Business address, or "service area only" if you go to customers
6. Opening hours
7. ABN / licence numbers you want displayed (e.g. plumbing or electrical licence)
8. Year you started

## Your customers
9. Describe your ideal customer in a sentence or two.
10. Which towns and suburbs do you want more work from?
11. What are the top 3 reasons customers choose you over competitors?
12. What questions do customers ask you most often?
13. Name 2–3 competitors (or websites you like the look of).

## Your services
14. List your main services, most important first.
15. Do you want prices shown on the site? (Yes / "From" prices / No)
16. Any specials, guarantees or discounts? (e.g. seniors' discount, free quotes)

## What the website should do
17. What's the #1 thing you want visitors to do? (Call / fill in a quote form / book online / visit the shop)
18. Do you use a booking system? (Fresha, Timely, Square Appointments, etc.)
19. Do you have a Google Business Profile? (Yes / No / Not sure)
20. Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)

## Look and feel
21. Do you have a logo? (Upload it, or "No")
22. Any brand colours? (Or "use the colours from my logo / van / shop")
23. Three words that describe how you want customers to feel about your business.

## Photos and content
24. Upload 5–20 photos: your work, your team, your shop or vehicle. Phone photos are fine; landscape works best.
25. Upload any reviews or testimonials you'd like used (or say "use my Google reviews").
26. Anything else we should know?

## Domain and access
27. Do you already own a domain (e.g. yourbusiness.com.au)? Who is it with?
28. Do you have an existing website we're replacing? Who hosts it?

---

**Tip:** if they're not good with forms, do it as a **15-minute phone call** and fill it in yourself while they talk.
