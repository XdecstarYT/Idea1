# 12 · 30-day launch plan

**Start:** Monday 26 October 2026 · **Finish:** Tuesday 24 November 2026
**Goal:** website live in week 1, outreach from week 2, **first paying client (signed) by day 30.**

**Your hours (as assumed):** Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 5–10pm · Sat and Sun 4–11pm · Wednesdays off.
**Legal calling windows inside those hours:** **weekdays 5–8pm** and **Saturday 4–5pm**. **No calls on Sundays or public holidays.**

> 💡 **Biggest lever:** tradies often answer their mobiles between 5 and 7pm, after work and before dinner, so that's your best calling window. If you can ever find **one daytime hour** (lunch break, day off), use it for calls to shops and cafés.

## Habits that make this work
- **Track every dial.** You can't improve what you don't count.
- **Speed wins.** Send mockups within 48 hours of a "yes".
- **Expect a lot of "no".** Nine out of ten saying no is normal. Every no gets you closer to a yes.
- **Ask every client for a Google review and a referral.** Referrals aren't cold sales, so there's no cooling-off delay.
