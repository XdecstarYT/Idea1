# 01 · Finding leads

**The goal:** a list of local businesses that would clearly benefit from a new website, each with a reason you can mention on the call.

## Where to find them (all free)

| Source | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Google Maps** | Search "plumber Warrnambool", "café Port Fairy", "hairdresser Koroit" and so on. Open each listing: no website link = hot lead. A website link = run the audit below. |
| **Facebook** | Search local business pages. Many tradies and salons *only* have Facebook. That's your opener. |
| **Yellow Pages / True Local / hipages / Oneflare** | Businesses listed here but with no website of their own. |
| **Local newspaper and noticeboards** | The local paper's classifieds and business ads, supermarket noticeboards, the backs of utes and vans. |
| **Instagram** | Salons, cafés and boutiques with good photos and nowhere to send people. |
| **New ABNs** | New businesses need websites. Watch for "now open" posts in local Facebook groups. |

**Towns to cover:** Warrnambool, Port Fairy, Koroit, Allansford, Dennington, Woodford, Terang, Camperdown, Mortlake, Timboon, Port Campbell, Peterborough, Hamilton and Portland.

**Industries, in priority order:**
1. **Trades:** plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, concreters, landscapers, fencers, painters, mechanics, cleaners. Each job is worth a lot, so one lead pays for the site.
2. **Tourism and hospitality:** cafés, restaurants, accommodation, tours. The Great Ocean Road brings in visitors searching on their phones.
3. **Hair, beauty and health:** salons, barbers, beauty therapists, massage, physios, PTs, dog groomers.
4. **Everyone else:** retail, professional services, community groups.

## 2-minute website audit (do this before every call or email)

Open their site **on your phone** and tick each one that fails:

- [ ] **No website at all** (Facebook only, or nothing)
- [ ] **Not mobile-friendly:** you have to pinch and zoom, or the text is tiny
- [ ] **Slow:** takes more than 3 seconds to load. Test it at pagespeed.web.dev; a mobile score under 50 is a selling point.
- [ ] **No tap-to-call button,** or the phone number is hard to find
- [ ] **Shows "Not secure":** the address starts with `http://`, not `https://`
- [ ] **Out of date:** old copyright year, old prices, dead links, "coming soon" pages
- [ ] **No Google Business Profile,** or one with no photos or hours
- [ ] **Few or no Google reviews** compared with their competitors
- [ ] **Doesn't show up** when you search "[their trade] [their town]"

**Score:** 3 or more ticks = **hot lead**. 1–2 = warm. 0 = skip (their site is fine, and say so if they ask).

Write the **single biggest problem** in your tracker. That's your opening line on the call:
> "I noticed when I searched 'electrician Port Fairy' on my phone, you didn't come up, but three others did."

## Daily lead target
- **Week 1:** build a list of 100 leads (around 20 a night).
- **From week 2:** add 10–15 new leads a night to keep the list full.

## Before you call any number
Check it against the **Do Not Call Register** (see `05-compliance-australia.md`). Mark it in the tracker: `DNC washed: Y` and the date.
