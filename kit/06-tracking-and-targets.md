# 06 · Tracker and targets

## Set up your tracker (5 minutes, free)
1. Open **Google Sheets** and create a new sheet.
2. **File → Import → Upload** `06-crm-tracker.csv`. This gives you the columns.
3. Select the **Status** column → **Data → Data validation → Dropdown** with these options:
   `New · Audited · Called – no answer · Voicemail · Contacted · Mockup booked · Mockup sent · Proposal sent · Cooling-off · Won · Lost · Nurture · Do not contact`
4. **Format → Conditional formatting:** Won = green, Do not contact = red, Mockup sent = yellow.
5. **View → Freeze → 1 row.**
6. Each evening, sort by **Next action date** so you know who's next.

## Status meanings
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | Found, not yet audited |
| Audited | Audit done, ready to contact |
| Called – no answer / Voicemail | Try again in 2–3 days, at a different time |
| Contacted | Spoke to them; note the outcome |
| Mockup booked | They said yes to a free mockup |
| Mockup sent | Waiting on feedback; chaser sequence running |
| Proposal sent | Agreement sent |
| Cooling-off | Signed; 10 business days before the deposit and build (for unsolicited sales) |
| Won | Paid deposit or first month |
| Lost | Said no; note why |
| Nurture | Not now. Check in again in 3 months |
| Do not contact | Asked not to be contacted. **Never contact again.** |

## Key numbers (track weekly)
Add a second tab called **"Weekly"** with these columns:

`Week | Leads added | Dials | Conversations | Emails sent | Mockups booked | Mockups sent | Proposals | Won | $ upfront | $ monthly`

**Typical conversion rates for cold outreach to small businesses (use them as a starting guide):**

```
100 dials → ~30 conversations → ~8 mockups booked → ~6 mockups sent → ~2 clients
```

So **about 50 dials ≈ 1 client.** Your first goal is learning which part of that chain is weakest:
- Few conversations → call at different times (5–7pm works well for tradies on their mobiles).
- Conversations but no mockups → practise the hook and the close.
- Mockups but no sales → improve the mockups, follow up faster, push the $0-upfront option.

## Weekly targets (with your 5–10pm schedule)
| | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leads added | 100 | 50 | 50 | 50 |
| Dials (5–8pm weekdays, Sat 4–5pm) | 0 | 40 | 60 | 60 |

| Mockups sent | 0 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| Clients won | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1–2 |

## Sunday review (15 minutes)
1. Fill in the Weekly tab.
2. Which step lost the most people? Pick **one** thing to change next week.
3. Plan next week's call list: 15–20 audited leads per calling night.
