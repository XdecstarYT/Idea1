# 09 · Service agreement outline

> ⚠️ **Get this reviewed before you use it.** This is a plain-English starting point, not legal advice. Free or cheap options: a **community legal centre**, Consumer Affairs Victoria's guides, or **Business Victoria's** templates. If the sale came from a cold call, cold email or walk-in, the **unsolicited consumer agreement** rules apply. See `05-compliance-australia.md` section 4. The cooling-off section below is written for that case.

Use **Google Docs + a free e-signature tool** (e.g. Docusign's free plan, or simply have them reply "I agree" by email and keep a PDF copy).

---

# Website Services Agreement

**Between:** Marshall Web Co., [Your full name], ABN [ABN], [Address], [Phone], [Email] ("**we**")
**And:** [Client business name], ABN [ABN], [Contact name], [Address] ("**you**")
**Date:** [Date]

## 1. What we'll do
We'll provide the **[package name]** package described in the attached proposal dated [date], including: [list].

## 2. Price and payment
- **Upfront option:** total $[amount]. 50% deposit ($[amount]) due [after the cooling-off period / on signing], balance ($[amount]) due on launch.
- **Monthly option:** $[amount] per month, first payment due on launch, then monthly in advance. **Minimum term: 12 months**, then month to month with 30 days' notice to cancel.
- No GST is charged (we're not registered for GST).
- Invoices are due within 7 days.

## 3. Timeline
We'll deliver a first draft within [X] days of receiving your content and deposit (if applicable). Delays in providing content extend the timeline.

## 4. Revisions
Two rounds of changes are included. Extra changes are quoted before we do them.

## 5. Your responsibilities
You'll provide accurate information and content, and confirm you have the right to use any photos, logos and text you give us.

## 6. Ownership
- **Upfront:** once paid in full, you own the website design and content.
- **Monthly:** you own your domain and content. You can buy out the design at any time for the remaining months of the minimum term (or $300 after the minimum term).
- You always own your domain. We'll register it in **your** name.
- We may show the site in our portfolio unless you tell us not to.

## 7. Hosting and care
[Included in monthly plans / Optional care plan at $[amount]/month]. We aim for reliable hosting but can't guarantee uninterrupted service or specific Google rankings.

## 8. Late payment
If a payment is more than 30 days overdue, we may pause the website until the account is paid.

## 9. Ending the agreement
- Either party may end the agreement with written notice if the other seriously breaches it and doesn't fix it within 14 days.
- On a monthly plan, cancelling during the minimum term means paying out the remaining months, or the buy-out amount in clause 6.

## 10. Cooling-off period (unsolicited agreements)
**Important notice to the client:** *You have the right to cancel this agreement within 10 business days from and including the day after you signed or received this agreement, for any reason. Details about your additional rights to cancel are set out in the attached cancellation information.* We won't take any payment or begin paid work during this period.

*(Attach the cancellation notice form required by the Australian Consumer Law. The ACCC website has the requirements.)*

## 11. Your consumer rights
Nothing in this agreement excludes, restricts or modifies any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.
