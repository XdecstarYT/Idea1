# 11 · Payments and invoicing

## Free setup
| Need | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices | **Zoho Invoice** (free plan) or a Google Docs template | $0 |
| Bank transfer | A separate everyday account just for the business (most banks offer them with no monthly fee) | $0 |
| Card payments and subscriptions | **Stripe** (payment links and subscriptions) or **Square** | No monthly fee, around 1.7–2% + 30c per card payment *(check current rates)* |
| Monthly direct debit | Stripe subscription, or Stripe's BECS Direct Debit (cheaper per payment) | Per payment only |

**Keep business money in a separate account from day one.** It makes tax time easy.

## What every invoice must include
- **"Invoice"** at the top, plus an invoice number (MWC-001, MWC-002…)
- Your name, **"Marshall Web Co."**, and your **ABN**
- Date issued and due date (7 days)
- The client's business name
- What it's for (e.g. "Growth website package, 50% deposit")
- Amount, and the line **"No GST has been charged"**
- How to pay: BSB, account number, account name, and/or a Stripe payment link

## Payment flow
**Upfront packages**
1. Agreement signed → wait for cooling-off to end (if unsolicited) → **50% deposit invoice**.
2. Build starts once the deposit arrives.
3. Launch day → **balance invoice**. Don't connect their domain until it's paid.

**$0-upfront monthly plans**
1. Agreement signed → build (after cooling-off, if it applies).
2. Launch day → set up a **Stripe subscription** (card or direct debit) starting that day.
3. Stripe charges them automatically every month and emails receipts.

**Care plans:** a Stripe subscription at $29 or $59 a month.

## Chasing late payments
- **Day 1 overdue:** friendly reminder. *"Hi [Name], just a heads-up that invoice MWC-00X was due yesterday. Here's the payment link: [link]. Thanks!"*
- **Day 7:** a phone call.
- **Day 14:** a firm email that mentions clause 8 (site pause after 30 days).
- **Day 30:** pause the site (for monthly plans), as the agreement allows.

## Money to set aside
- **Tax:** put **25%** of every payment into a separate savings account.
- **Reinvest:** from the first deposit, pay for your **domain** (~$25 a year) and **business name** (~$45 a year).
- **GST:** keep an eye on turnover. You must register when it reaches **$75k a year**.
