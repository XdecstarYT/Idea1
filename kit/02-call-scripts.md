# 02 · Call scripts

**Rules for every call:**
- Your caller ID must be **on**. Never call from a blocked number.
- Say your name, your business and why you're calling in the first few seconds.
- If they say "don't call me", end politely and add them to your do-not-call list **straight away**.
- Calls only between **9am–8pm weekdays** and **9am–5pm Saturday**. **Never on Sundays or national public holidays.**
- **Goal of the call: book a free mockup, not sell a website.** The mockup sells the website.

---

## 1. Opener (first 10 seconds)

> "Hi, is that **[Owner name]**? … G'day [Name], it's **[Your name] from Marshall Web Co.** here in Warrnambool. I'll be quick, is now a bad time?"

Asking "is now a *bad* time?" gets a better response than "is now a good time?". Most people say "no, go on".

- **If it's a bad time:** "No worries at all. When's better, tomorrow arvo or Thursday?" Book it and hang up.
- **If you don't know the owner's name:** "Hi, who's the best person to talk to about your website or Google listing?"

## 2. Hook (why you called them specifically)

Use the biggest problem from your audit:

- **No website:** "I was searching for **[trade] in [town]** on my phone and noticed you've got a great Facebook page and good reviews, but no website. So anyone who Googles first is ending up with your competitors."
- **Old or slow site:** "I had a look at your website on my phone. It takes about **[X] seconds** to load and the call button's hard to find. That usually means people give up and ring the next business."
- **Not on Google Maps:** "When I searched '**[trade] near me**' in [town], three businesses came up on the map but you weren't one of them."

Then: **"Is getting more [jobs / bookings / customers] something you're keen on at the moment?"**

## 3. Discovery (2–3 questions, then listen)

1. "Where do most of your new customers come from at the moment?"
2. "If you had **five more [jobs/bookings] a month**, would you have room for them?"
3. "Have you thought about a website before? What stopped you?" (This reveals the objection early.)

## 4. Value pitch (keep it under 30 seconds)

> "Here's what I do. I build websites for local businesses: fast, easy to use on a phone, with a big tap-to-call button, and set up properly on Google Maps so people searching '[trade] near me' find you. It's live in about a week or two, and you can pay upfront or **start from nothing upfront** with a monthly plan."

## 5. Close: the free mockup

> "What I'd love to do is **design your homepage for free**, so you can see exactly what it'd look like. No cost, no obligation. If you don't love it, you don't pay a cent. Would that be worth a look?"

**If yes:**
> "Great. What's the best email or mobile to send it to? And can I grab 10 minutes on **[day]** to show you? I can come by in person or do it over the phone, whatever suits."

Confirm the day, the time and how you'll show it. Write it all in the tracker straight away.

**Then send the "after call" message** from `04-follow-up-templates.md` within 1 hour.

## 6. Voicemail (under 20 seconds)

> "Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from Marshall Web Co. in Warrnambool. I was searching for [trade] in [town] and noticed something about your Google listing that's probably costing you calls. I'd love to show you. It's free. My number's **[number]**, that's [number] again. Cheers."

Leave a voicemail **once**. Next time, try a different time of day or send an email instead.

## 7. Getting past the person who answers

> "Hi, it's [Your name] from Marshall Web Co. Could you put me through to **[Owner]**? It's about their Google listing."

If they ask what it's about:
> "I've got a quick idea for getting them more calls from Google. Is there a better time to catch them?"

Be friendly and get their name. They're your ally.

## 8. Walk-in script (in person, Saturdays)

> "Hi, I'm [Your name] from Marshall Web Co. I'm local and I build websites for businesses around Warrnambool. I won't take up your time while you're busy. Is the owner in? … I've done a quick mock-up of what your homepage could look like. Can I show you for 30 seconds?" (Show it on your phone.)

Leave a card or a flyer with a QR code linking to your site. Walking into shops counts as unsolicited selling, so the same rules apply. See `05-compliance-australia.md`.

## Call checklist (keep open while dialling)
- [ ] Number washed against the Do Not Call Register
- [ ] Audit done; biggest problem written down
- [ ] Opener → hook → 2–3 questions → pitch → mockup close
- [ ] Next step booked (day and time), or marked "no"
- [ ] Tracker updated before the next call
