# 04 · Follow-up templates (email and SMS)

**Spam Act rules for every marketing email or SMS:**
- **Consent:** only send to businesses that gave permission (on a call, through your form) **or** whose email or mobile is clearly published (on their website, Google listing or Facebook), as long as your message is about their business and they haven't said "no unsolicited messages".
- **Identify yourself:** your name, business name and contact details in every message.
- **Unsubscribe:** every message must include an easy way to opt out. Act on opt-outs within **5 business days**.

Replace everything in `[BRACKETS]`.

## C. No-response chaser (3 touches)

**Touch 1 (2 days after the mockup) · SMS**
> Hi [Name], [Your name] from Marshall Web Co. here. Did you get a chance to look at your homepage mockup? Here's the link again: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Touch 2 (5 days after the mockup) · Email**
**Subject:** Quick question, [Name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Just checking the mockup landed OK. Is it:
> 1. Looks great, let's talk
> 2. Like it, but want changes
> 3. Not right now
>
> Just reply with a number. Any answer is fine, honestly.
>
> [Signature]

**Touch 3 (10 days after the mockup) · Email, the final one**
**Subject:** Should I close your file?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I haven't heard back, so I'll assume now's not the right time, and that's completely fine. I'll stop following up.
>
> The mockup's yours to keep: [link]. If you ever want to pick it back up, just reply to this email.
>
> All the best with the business,
> [Signature]

After touch 3: mark the lead "Nurture" in the tracker and check in once, **3 months later**.
