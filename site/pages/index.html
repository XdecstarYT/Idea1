Questions

Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Email me and I'll reply within a day.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I design your homepage before you commit to anything. If it's not right for you, you don't pay and you don't owe me anything.

How does $0 upfront work?

Instead of paying for the website in one go, you pay a monthly fee that also covers hosting, security and small changes. There's a 12-month minimum, then it's month to month.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Absolutely. Run the free website check above, or I'll review it for you and tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild, a tidy-up, or is fine as it is.

Do I own my website?

If you pay upfront, yes, it's yours. On monthly plans you own your domain and content, and you can buy out the design at any time.

I'm not good with tech. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what I'm for. Answer a short questionnaire, send some photos, and I handle the rest.

How long does it take?

Your free mockup takes about 3 days. Starter sites go live in about 7 days and Growth in about 14, depending on how quickly we get your photos and details.
