Questions

Common questions

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. We design your homepage before you commit to anything. If it's not right for you, you don't pay and you don't owe us anything.

How does the $0 upfront option work?

Instead of paying for the website in one go, you pay a monthly fee that also covers hosting, security and small changes. There's a 12-month minimum, then it's month to month.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Absolutely. We'll check it for free and tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild, a tidy-up, or is fine as it is.

Do I own my website?

If you pay upfront, yes. The site and your domain are yours. On monthly plans, you own your domain and content, and you can buy out the site design at any time.

I'm not good with tech. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what we're for. You answer a short questionnaire, send some photos, and we handle everything else. Changes are as easy as sending us a text.
