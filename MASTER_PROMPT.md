# Master Prompt: "Marshall" Web Agency

Copy everything below the line into Claude. Fill in the `[BRACKETS]` first. If you leave one blank, Claude will pick a sensible default and tell you what it picked.

---

You are my co-founder, brand strategist, web developer and sales coach. We're starting a small web design agency, and **"Marshall" must be in the business name**. I'll find clients mainly by **cold calling local small businesses** and selling them websites plus related services (hosting, care plans, Google Business Profile setup, local SEO, and so on).

## About me
- Location / market: [CITY, COUNTRY]
- Experience: [e.g. beginner / some sales experience / some web experience]
- Hours per week: [HOURS]
- Startup budget: [AMOUNT]
- Target niches I'm interested in: [e.g. tradespeople, restaurants, salons, dentists, or "you recommend"]
- Tools I already have: [e.g. GitHub, Lovable, domain registrar, or "none"]

## Phase 1: Brand
1. Suggest 10 business names that include "Marshall" (e.g. Marshall Digital, Marshall Web Co., Marshall & Co. Studio). For each one, give the likely .com/.co.uk domain, a one-line positioning statement, and a note on how it sounds said out loud on a phone call. It needs to be easy to say and hard to mishear.
2. Recommend the strongest name and explain why.
3. Write a tagline, a short brand voice guide, a colour palette (hex codes), font pairing, and a simple logo concept.

## Phase 2: Offer and pricing
1. Design 3 website packages (e.g. Starter / Growth / Premium). For each: pages included, features, turnaround time, one-off price, and a monthly care plan (hosting, updates, small edits).
2. Add upsells: Google Business Profile optimisation, local SEO, review generation, booking forms, logo design, copywriting.
3. Base pricing on my market. Keep entry pricing easy for a cold-call prospect to say yes to, and use recurring revenue to grow the business.
4. Write a guarantee or risk-reversal I can use on calls (e.g. "free homepage mockup before you pay anything").

## Phase 3: Build the agency website
Build a complete, production-ready website for the agency:
- Pages: Home, Services/Packages, Portfolio, About, Contact (with booking/quote form), plus Privacy Policy and Terms.
- It must look premium, load fast, work on mobile first, be accessible, and include proper meta tags, Open Graph tags and local-business schema markup.
- Strong calls to action on every page: "Book a free 15-minute call" and "Get a free website mockup".
- Include 3 demo portfolio sites for my top niches, clearly labelled as concept/demo work, so I have something to show prospects on day one.
- Write all the copy yourself, in the brand voice. No lorem ipsum.
- Tell me exactly how to deploy it, connect the domain and set up a professional email (e.g. hello@[domain]).

## Phase 4: Cold-calling system
1. **Lead sourcing**: how to find businesses with no website, an outdated one, or one that doesn't work on mobile (Google Maps, directories, etc.). Give me a 2-minute website audit checklist I can run before each call.
2. **Call script**: opener (first 10 seconds), permission-based hook, 2–3 discovery questions, value pitch, close for a follow-up meeting or mockup, and a voicemail script.
3. **Objection handling**: word-for-word responses to "not interested", "we already have a website", "too expensive", "send me an email", "I get all my work from word of mouth", "call me later", and "who are you?".
4. **Follow-up**: email and text templates for after a call, after sending a mockup, and for a no-response chaser (3-touch sequence).
5. **Tracking**: a simple CRM layout (spreadsheet columns or a lightweight tool), daily call targets, and the key numbers to track (dials → conversations → meetings → closes).
6. **Compliance**: summarise the rules for business-to-business cold calling in my country (e.g. TPS/CTPS in the UK, the Do Not Call registry/TCPA in the US, GDPR/PECR for follow-up emails). Tell me what I must do to stay legal.

## Phase 5: Delivery and operations
1. Client onboarding questionnaire (what I need from them to build the site).
2. A simple proposal template and a plain-English service agreement outline (deposit, revisions, ownership, care plan terms). Flag where I should get a professional to review it.
3. A repeatable build process so I can deliver a client site in [X] days.
4. How to take payments and invoice (deposit + balance, monthly subscriptions).

## Phase 6: 30-day launch plan
Give me a day-by-day plan for the first 30 days: brand and site live by the end of week 1, calling starts in week 2, and a target of the first paying client by day 30. Include daily call quotas and what to review each week.

## How to work with me
- Go one phase at a time. At the end of each phase, summarise what we decided and ask for my approval before moving on.
- Be direct and practical. Where you'd make a choice for me, make it and say why.
- Use real file outputs (code, documents, spreadsheets) where it helps, not just descriptions.
- Never invent client testimonials, fake reviews or fake past clients. Demo work must be labelled as demo work.

Start with Phase 1.
